About BTC Checker

Version 2.5 • Public Explorer

This app allows checking balances for up to 50 public Bitcoin addresses simultaneously using open public APIs from Blockchain.info and Mempool.space, with live multi-fiat exchange rates.

  • 100% Secure: No Private Keys ever needed or stored.
  • Supports Legacy, SegWit, Bech32 & Taproot address formats.
  • Live fiat exchange rates for 13 major global currencies.
